About Khan Sadat Hosen

Hello, I’m Khan Sadat Hosen, a passionate Digital Marketing, SEO, and Web Development Specialist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I have hands-on experience in creating user-friendly websites, improving search engine rankings, and running digital campaigns that help businesses grow online. My focus is always on delivering practical, measurable, and professional results.

I completed certified training in Digital Marketing from the National Institute of Youth Development (NIYD), Bangladesh, where I developed practical knowledge in SEO, social media marketing, online advertising, and campaign strategy.

Currently, I am also pursuing my Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Botany at Satkhira Government College, Bangladesh.

I am a dedicated professional who believes in continuous learning, discipline, and high-quality work.
